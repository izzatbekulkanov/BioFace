Google Play Marketga yuklash qadamlari

Yig'ilgan yangi AAB faylini do'konga joylashtirish tartibi.

1

Play Console dashboardiga kiring

Google Play Console portalida o'z hisobingizga kiring va BioFace Portal ilovasini tanlang.

2

Yangi Nashr Yaratish (Create Release)

Chap tomondagi menyudan Production bo'limiga o'ting va o'ng yuqoridagi Create new release tugmasini bosing.

3

AAB faylni yuklang

Yuklab olingan yangi app-release.aab faylini sudrab olib kelib yoki yuklash orqali "App bundles" bo'limiga qo'shing. Google avtomatik tarzda versiya kodini 1.0.2 (3) deb aniqlaydi.

4

Release Notes va Rollout

Release notes qismiga ilovadagi o'zgarishlar (masalan: Yangi tezkor interfeys, sozlamalar sahifasi va optimallashtirishlar) deb yozing. So'ngra Next -> Save -> Start rollout to Production tugmasini bosing. Ilova tekshiruvga yuboriladi.

Apple App Store (iPhone) ga chiqarish qadamlari

iOS ilovasini yig'ish va App Store Connect-ga joylash tartibi (macOS tizimida bajariladi).

Muhim ma'lumot (Windows cheklovi)

Apple kompaniyasi qoidalariga ko'ra, iOS ilovalarini build qilish va imzolash uchun macOS tizimi (MacBook/Mac mini) va Xcode dasturi bo'lishi shart. Agar sizda Mac bo'lmasa, **Codemagic.io** yoki **GitHub Actions** kabi bulutli xizmatlar orqali build qilish mumkin.

1

App Store Connect-da ilova ochish

App Store Connect portaliga kiring, My Apps -> + (New App) tugmasini bosing va ma'lumotlarni to'ldiring:

  • Platforma: iOS. Nomi: BioFace Portal.
  • Bundle ID: Loyihangiz Bundle identifikatorini tanlang (masalan, com.bioface.portal).
  • SKU: Ixtiyoriy noyob satr kiritiladi (masalan: bioface-portal-sku).
2

Loyihani Xcode-da ochish (Mac-da)

Loyiha ichidagi ios/Runner.xcworkspace faylini Xcode-da oching:

  • Signing & Capabilities bo'limiga kiring va o'z Apple Developer profilingizni tanlang ("Automatically manage signing" yoqing).
  • Versiya va Build raqamlarini moslang (Version: 1.0.2, Build: 3).
3

iOS build yig'ish va yuklash

Loyihangiz terminalida build buyrug'ini bering, so'ng Xcode orqali arxivlang:

flutter build ipa --release

Build tugagach, Xcode-ning Product -> Archive -> Distribute App -> App Store Connect -> Upload ketma-ketligi orqali ilovani to'g'ridan-to'g'ri Apple serverlariga yuklang.

4

Test va Tekshiruvga yuborish

Ilova yuklangach, App Store Connect-da **iOS App** bo'limida yangi yuklangan buildni tanlang, test hisob ma'lumotlarini to'ldiring va **Submit for Review** (Tekshiruvga yuborish) tugmasini bosing.

Maxfiylik Savolnomalari

Har ikkala do'kon so'raydigan maxfiylik va ma'lumotlar xavfsizligi bo'yicha to'g'ri javoblar ko'rsatmasi.

A) Google Play - Data Safety

  • Joylashuv (Location): Precision & Approximate coordinates to'planadi (ish vaqtida monitoring uchun). Boshqalarga ulashilmaydi.
  • Shaxsiy ma'lumotlar (Personal Info): Xodim ID va ismi to'planadi (identifikatsiya uchun).
  • Rasm (Photos): Kirish-chiqish yuz tekshiruvi uchun kamera orqali rasm olinadi va shifrlangan tarzda serverga yuboriladi.

B) Apple App Store - App Privacy

App Store Connect-da **App Privacy** bo'limini to'ldirganda quyidagi toifadagi ma'lumotlar yig'ilishini belgilang:

  • Location (Precise Location): Ha. Foydalanish maqsadi: App Functionality (ish soatlarini hisoblash).
  • Contact Info (Name): Ha. Foydalanish maqsadi: Account Setup / App Functionality.
  • User Content (Photos): Ha. Foydalanish maqsadi: App Functionality (biometrik davomat).
  • Identifiers (Device ID): Ha. Foydalanish maqsadi: Security / App Access (akkauntni qurilmaga bog'lash).

Ilova yangilanganda versiyalarni hisoblash va to'g'ri yangilash tartib-qoidalari.

Versiya raqami tuzilishi (pubspec.yaml)

Flutterda versiya raqami version: 1.0.2+3 ko'rinishida yoziladi:

  • 1.0.2 (Version Name): Foydalanuvchilarga do'konda ko'rinadigan chiroyli matnli versiya raqami.
  • 3 (Version Code): Ichki butun sonli raqam. Do'konga har safar yangilanish yuklanganda, ushbu kod strictly oldingisidan kattaroq bo'lishi shart (masalan, keyingi safar 4, keyin 5 va h.k.). Aks holda, do'konlar uni qabul qilmaydi.
